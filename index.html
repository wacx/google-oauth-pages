cc2obs-fitbit

cc2obs-fitbit is a personal, single-user integration between a private Discord bot and the Google Health API. It records the developer's own workout sessions (exercise type, duration, calories) into the Google Health API on behalf of the developer only, so they appear alongside data from the developer's own fitness devices and apps.

This is not a public product or service. It is not distributed to, or intended for use by, anyone other than its developer. There is no sign-up, no other accounts, and no other users.

See the Privacy Policy for details on data handling.
